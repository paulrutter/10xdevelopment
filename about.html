About

Hi, I'm Paul Rütter. I'm Director of Technology at BlueConic, a customer data platform. My work sits where engineering meets leadership: designing systems that stay simple as they grow, building teams that move fast without breaking things, and shipping software that actually matters to the people using it.

Outside the day job, I'm a PMC member of Apache Felix, focused on the HTTP and Maven Bundle Plugin subprojects. I maintain a couple of open-source libraries that came out of real production needs — node-oom-heapdump for hunting down Node.js memory leaks, and browscap-java for fast user-agent detection — and I contribute to other projects across the ecosystem, including Eclipse Jersey and DuckDB, among others. Lately I've been tinkering with agentic AI — there's a selection on the projects page.

The "10x" here isn't about heroics. It's about leverage: the right tool, a clean abstraction, or a bit of automation that makes everything after it easier. This site is a small example — a fast, static personal home built on a deliberately lean stack.
